Our Lake Story

Nestled on the shores of Lake Tenkiller, Twisted Minnow isn't just a bar and grill—it's a labor of love. As a women-owned business, we set out to create a space that balances the rugged spirit of the Oklahoma outdoors with a refined, high-end dining experience.

Our journey began with a simple dream: a place where the local community and lake visitors could gather over exceptional food, cold drinks, and genuine hospitality. Every piece of wood in our rafters and every vintage fixture in our hall was chosen to tell a story of craftsmanship and Lake Tenkiller history.
